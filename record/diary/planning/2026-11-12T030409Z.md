Two follow-ups:
- Record-only PRs: today each one downloads the server and runs the whole smoke test, a few minutes, and
  fails if anything outside the PR breaks (say, the game's download host is down). The fix is a step
  inside the one `build` job that skips the heavy steps when only `record/` changed (not a
  workflow-level `paths` filter, which would leave the required check pending forever). Nobody owns
  `.github/` until the Road Bible's shared list names a thread, so I propose there that the proto thread
  owns it, as SETUP suggests, and makes this change as part of the prototype.
- PR identity: because threads open and merge PRs under the owner's login, the history cannot tell the
  owner's merge from a thread's. SETUP gives two answers: a separate account or app for threads, or the
  list of owner-merge PRs the inquisitor sends at each step gate (rule 7). This is question 5 below.

## The hand-over, in order

1. **The rules land.** The owner merges this PR. Then the coordinator tells every thread (rule 4).
2. **The coordinator's files.** The coordinator creates `record/rulings.md` and `record/player-diary.md`,
   and logs questions 1 to 4 below as the first entries. The owner's vision, which the Road Bible is written
   from, is "set down by the owner, written into rulings.md by the coordinator in the owner's own words"
   (THE RECORD), so the owner's earlier rulings must reach rulings.md before the Road Bible can rest on
   them. That is question 3.
3. **The review thread is created** once the rules are on main, so that it exists before the first Road
   Bible is drafted (rule 4: "read by the review thread, then the owner's merge"). The inquisitor, the
   janitor and the builders are not needed yet: the inquisitor first decides something at the first patch
   or step gate, the janitor only when an originator cannot fix its code, the proto thread starts once the
   first Road Bible merges, and the builders' role names come from the Road Bible.
4. **The old work becomes history, not standing.** The continuation plan, its threads table, review 1 and
   review 2's candidates, and post-mortem proposals 1 to 14 are not carried as rules or steps. Where they
   hold evidence (the players' "weak when hunted" reports, the unread `ConfigVersion` field that a changed
   default will need, the audit's scope), the Road Bible draft takes them up as proposed steps and cites
   them, and the review thread can read them where they lie. The old diaries stay where they are: no thread
   may write another thread's diary, and copying them into record/diary/ would do that.
5. **Repo instructions that disagree with version 2.** `CLAUDE.md` lets "a small fix inside one feature's
   files with no design change" auto-merge on green CI, and sends threads to README "Working in parallel"
   for what they own. Version 2 lets a patch auto-merge only on the inquisitor's "in", and ownership comes
   from the Road Bible. The project instructions also still carry the old rule 6. That is question 4.
6. **The first Road Bible.** This thread drafts it once questions 1 to 4 are answered: the concept (from the
   owner's 1.2 direction of 2026-11-10 and the rulings carried in), the prototype's terms and the proto
   thread's folder, the steps and features with an owning thread and folders each (the shipped features get
   numbers too), and the shared list (CLAUDE.md, README, CHANGELOG, `.github/`, `build.sh`, `CakeBuild/`,
   `scripts/`, `Walkers/Core/`, the shared files in `Walkers/Config/`, `modinfo.json`, `docs/`). The
   workshop's four open questions are about the concept, so they go to the owner with the draft, not now.
   The coordinator then wakes the review thread (rule 6 (a)), and the owner reviews it with the review
   thread's report and merges it.
7. **Then the proto thread starts** (rule 2, rule 3), on the terms question 1 settles.

## Questions for the owner (rule 1)

Each of these has two readings that lead to different work, and each is a reading of the rules, so under
rule 1 it is a stop for everyone but the coordinator and the review thread. No other thread exists yet, so
in practice this thread alone stops: the Road Bible waits for the answers. Each has my recommendation.

1. **What is the prototype for a mod that already loads?** Rule 3 and rule 7 speak of "the prototype, the
   first build of the project that loads"; 1.1.1 already loads.
   (a) The Road Bible sets prototype terms for a new build (the smallest playable showing the 1.2
   direction), the proto thread builds it on top of 1.1.1, the owner gets the early zip, and step 1 writes
   no code until it merges.
   (b) 1.1.1 on main counts as the prototype, there is no proto thread, and step 1 may start code once the
   Road Bible and its briefs are merged.
   I recommend (a): it keeps the owner's play-test early, and someone must still own the checks.
2. **Do the shipped features need briefs?** The five features of 1.1.1 were built before rule 2. A patch is
   "in" only if it "leaves that feature's brief true as written"; with no brief, every patch is in doubt, so
   "out". (a) Each feature's owning builder writes a brief of its feature as it stands, for the owner's
   merge, before it changes that feature's code. (b) Shipped features stand without briefs, and every
   change to them waits for the owner's merge until a 1.2 brief covers them.
   I recommend (a).
3. **Do your earlier rulings stand?** Project memory holds your decisions and rulings of 2026-10 and
   2026-11 (settled design decisions, your answers on the creature, the 1.2 direction "Faster, however
   additional tweaks to monster stats and intelligence will need a work-shop" with the Super Predator
   idea). (a) They stand, and the coordinator carries each into rulings.md in your own words, marked as
   given before version 2; any that disagree with version 2 fall. (b) They are history, and the Road Bible
   starts from what you say now. I recommend (a).
4. **Which instructions win until CLAUDE.md is changed?** (a) Version 2 wins from the moment it merges; the
   shared list names a thread to bring CLAUDE.md and README in line (I propose the proto thread, inside
   the prototype); and you replace the project instructions' old rule 6 with one line pointing at
   record/COMMANDMENTS.txt. (b) CLAUDE.md is changed first, by your merge, before any other thread starts.
   I recommend (a).

## Why this way, and not another

- Why ask now rather than draft the Road Bible on my own readings: the coordinator's note and rule 1 both
  say a reading of the rules is not a thread's to choose, and the answers to 1 and 2 change the Road
  Bible's prototype terms and steps throughout. A draft on a guess would be rewritten.
- Why not write my map yet: a map lists the thread's features by Road Bible number, and writing it wakes
  the review thread (rule 6 (f)). The planning thread builds nothing and owns no feature, so its map waits
  for the Road Bible's numbers.
- Why everything rides one PR: this thread may push to one branch only, so
  rule 5's order ("every other entry ... goes to main at once by a record-only PR") cannot be kept
  while the rules' own PR is open. This entry and the "Next:" entry ride with the rules and reach main with
  them. Once record/ is on main, each later entry goes in its own record-only PR as rule 5 asks.
