# planning: map

The map of the planning thread (rule 5). It points to the Road Bible, `record/road-bible.md`, and adds
planning's own plan beneath. The Road Bible and the settled briefs outrank it: where this map says
anything different, the map is wrong.

Last written 2026-11-12 17:12Z, against the Road Bible as it stands on `main` at commit `cd2a49c`, the
owner's merge of PR #79.

## Features

None. Planning builds nothing (THE CAST). The Road Bible gives it no feature in part 3 and no row in
part 4. What planning writes is in `record/`: the Road Bible and every change to it, every change to
`record/COMMANDMENTS.txt` at the owner's word, and its own folder, `record/diary/planning/`.

## What planning needs, and from whom

- **The coordinator.** The owner's rulings and commands on the concept, the road and the rules, as
  entries of `record/rulings.md`, word for word. What the owner said after playing, where an entry of
  `record/player-diary.md` calls for a change to the book (rule 3). Every report and finding of the
  review thread on a PR of planning's. The wake of the review thread when planning reports such a PR
  ready (rule 6). Word of whether a record thread is at hand (THE CAST).
- **The record thread.** The owner's words in `record/rulings.md` and `record/player-diary.md`, so that
  the book can cite an entry by its number.
- **The review thread.** Its read of every change to the Road Bible and to the rules before the change
  merges, and its certificate on a change to the steps and features (rules 4 and 6).
- **The proto thread and the builder threads.** Word, through the coordinator, when one of them thinks
  the Road Bible wrong (rule 5). And word of a path that falls under no feature's folders and no row of
  part 4, so that planning adds it to the shared list before anyone creates it (part 4).
- **The inquisitor.** Nothing directly. What a gate shows about the next step reaches planning through
  the review thread's report on that gate and the coordinator (rule 6).

## Who needs something from planning

- **Every thread.** The Road Bible, true to `record/rulings.md`. Where a ruling changes what the book
  says, the book is changed first, before any code follows the ruling (rule 1).
- **The proto thread.** Part 2, the prototype's terms, and its rows in part 4.
- **The builder threads** `director`, `creature`, `harass` and `commands`. Part 3: their features, by
  number, with their folders. For `commands`, also its row in part 4.
- **The inquisitor.** Part 3, which says which features stand under which step (rule 7).
- **The review thread.** Each change to the book or to the rules as a ready PR that carries planning's
  diary entry, planning's answers to its findings by number, and this map.
- **The coordinator.** Word when a PR of planning's is ready, with its number and commit. Word when a
  change to the Road Bible has merged, and which parts, features, folders or files it changed, so that
  the coordinator can tell the threads concerned (rule 5).

## Planning's own plan

Planning may rewrite this part whenever it likes (rule 5). In the order planning expects the work:

1. **Write the owner's answer on the prototype's numbers into the book.** The owner answered R40 with
   (a): the numbers live in the prototype's own folder, as built (R45). Part 2 said otherwise, so two
   of its paragraphs change, in PR #105. The change touches the prototype's terms, so it goes by the
   review thread's read and the owner's merge (rule 4). It merges before the prototype, PR #75
   (rule 1).
2. **Follow the owner's word on the order of the road.** The review thread's counter-view to it (G22)
   is with the owner. If the owner reorders the road or moves feature 7, planning changes part 3.
3. **After the owner has played the prototype.** Where an entry of `record/player-diary.md` calls for a
   change to the Road Bible, planning writes that change before any code follows it (rule 3).
4. **At the book's next change, whatever calls for it,** planning weighs four things with it. None of
   them holds any work, and none is begun:
   - At the "Done" line of step 1, a sentence saying that a settings file which still holds the
     section `proto` is not among the files that line speaks of. The prototype's build writes that
     section into the settings file of an installation it runs on, and such a file gets no new
     settings added until the section is deleted (G40; the proto thread's map, step 8).
   - In the row of feature 9, that "the numbers as they stand" are the settings and the numbers fixed
     in the code alike (G42).
   - In the rows of features 8 and 9, that the thread brings to its brief what the prototype tried,
     as the proto thread's diary holds it. What the owner says after playing is said of those
     numbers, and once the prototype's folder is removed they are no longer in the code (R45). The
     review thread asked it of feature 9 when it read PR #105.
   - Whether the numbers of the gaze rule stay in point 5 of the concept. Planning weighs that again
     once the brief of feature 2 as it stands is settled.
5. **After the workshop of step 2.** The brief of feature 9 holds what the workshop settles about the
   four gaze cases (R34). Planning then brings point 5 of the concept in line with it, as the book
   says.
6. **After each step's gate.** The review thread says whether what was learned changes the next step
   (rule 6). Where the owner then rules or commands a change to the road, planning writes it.
7. **Before step 3 begins.** The owner ruled "tentatively" that reinforced blocks and locked or barred
   doors hold against the Hunter (R23), and the book builds nothing on it before step 3. When step 2
   nears its gate, planning asks the owner, through the coordinator, whether R23 stands, and writes the
   answer into the book before a brief of step 3 is begun.
8. **An audit, only if the owner asks for one.** The owner's words "Audit and correct any found issues,
   sure." are read as leave to correct what is found along the way, and not as an order for an audit
   as a piece of work of its own. The reading is recorded as R44 and stands until the owner says it is
   wrong. So no audit is on the road. If the owner wants one, planning writes it into part 3: what it
   reads, which thread does it, and under which step.
9. **The rules.** Planning writes a change to them only at the owner's word (THE CAST). None is asked
   for now.
